TruthGuard AI Methodology & NLP Principles

TruthGuard AI classifies claims through mathematical stylometry. Fake and misleading news often exhibits high emotional punctuation (!!!), extreme superlatives ("shocking", "miracle", "cabal"), and urgency cues, while authentic journalism prioritizes formal attribution ("according to researchers", "peer-reviewed study").
